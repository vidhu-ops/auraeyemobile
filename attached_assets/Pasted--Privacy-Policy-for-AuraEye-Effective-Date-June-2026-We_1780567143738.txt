## 1. Introduction

AuraEye™ ("AuraEye", "we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, share, and protect information when you use the AuraEye mobile application, website, and related services.

By accessing or using AuraEye, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

* Username
* Email address
* Password (stored securely using industry-standard encryption or hashing methods)
* Profile information voluntarily provided by the user

### Images and Uploaded Content

AuraEye allows users to upload images and photos for analysis.

Images uploaded by users are stored within the user's account and profile to provide ongoing access to generated reports and application features.

### Generated Reports

AuraEye generates reports and analysis results based on user-submitted content.

Generated reports are stored within the user's account so they can access previous analyses and purchased services.

### Device Information

We may collect:

* Device type
* Operating system version
* Device identifiers
* IP address
* Browser information
* Diagnostic information
* Error and crash information

### Transaction Information

When users purchase credits, reports, subscriptions, or other services, we may collect:

* Transaction identifiers
* Purchase history
* Credit balances
* Payment confirmation records
* Subscription status

AuraEye does not store complete payment card information.

Payments are processed through authorized third-party payment providers.

## 3. Permissions We Request

AuraEye may request camera access to allow users to capture photos directly within the application.

### Storage and Media Access

AuraEye may request permission to access photos and media files so users can upload images for analysis.

AuraEye uses artificial intelligence technologies, including services provided by OpenAI and Google Gemini, to analyze user-submitted content and generate reports.

Users acknowledge that AI-generated reports are automated outputs and should not be considered medical, legal, financial, psychological, or professional advice.

## 10. Data Retention

AuraEye retains account information while the account remains active.

Uploaded images and generated reports remain stored within the user's account until deleted by the user or until the account is deleted.

Transaction records, purchase records, and payment-related information may be retained for up to seven (7) years to comply with accounting, taxation, legal, fraud-prevention, and regulatory requirements.

Diagnostic and security logs may be retained for up to twelve (12) months.

## 11. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and personal information by contacting:

[admin@auraeyesolutions.com](mailto:admin@auraeyesolutions.com)

Deletion requests should include the user's registered email address.

Verified deletion requests will be processed within thirty (30) days.

Upon account deletion:

* User profile information will be removed or anonymized.
* Uploaded images will be deleted.
* Stored reports will be deleted.
* Future access to the account will be disabled.

Certain records may be retained where required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, taxation requirements, or regulatory obligations.
